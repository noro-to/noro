# Cross-functional partner

**Who:** a general reader anywhere in the org (brand, category, finance, ops). Knows the business, not the ad platforms.

**Checks:**
1. Is it clear to a generalist, with no assumed expertise?
2. Is the business impact obvious?
3. Is the context there: date range, comparison, what changed?

**Always sends back:**
- jargon only one team understands
- missing context that leaves the reader guessing
- results that stop at the metric without saying what it means for the business

**Note format:** quote the exact words, then the fix.
