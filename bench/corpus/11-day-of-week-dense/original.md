# Day-of-week performance

Since May, every day except Sunday has run between $95 and $109 per conversion; Sunday is at $138.

**Sunday is now the weak day: $138 per conversion since May vs. $101 on other days**

Sunday's conversion rate fell to 9.8% in May–August against 14.1% on other days, while its CPC ($13.52) stayed in line with the rest of the week. That points to what happens after the click, such as call handling or availability, rather than ad cost; it is a hypothesis for the client to confirm. Thursday's January–April gap has closed, and weekends overall take 22% of spend and 22% of conversions.
