# Weekly Google Ads Report: Kilnworth Coffee Roasters

**Reporting period:** Oct 5–11 vs. Sep 28–Oct 4

## Executive Summary

This week, the account continued to deliver steady, consistent results, demonstrating the continued effectiveness of our current strategy. Performance remained stable across all key metrics, reflecting a healthy and well-balanced account.

## Performance Overview

- **Spend:** $5,240 (vs. $5,180, +1.2%)
- **Purchases:** 188 (vs. 184, +2.2%)
- **Revenue:** $21,590 (vs. $20,980, +2.9%)
- **ROAS:** 4.12 (vs. 4.05), against a target ROAS of 380%
- **CPA:** $27.87 (vs. $28.15)
- **New subscriptions:** 41 (vs. 39)

## Campaign Deep Dive

PMax – Whole Bean & Subscriptions remained the primary driver of volume with 112 purchases. Search – Brand continued to deliver efficient results with 49 purchases, while Shopping – Gear & Brewers contributed 27.

## Device Insights

Mobile accounted for 64% of spend, a slight increase from the prior week. Notably, tablet conversion rate declined to 1.9% from 2.4% (6 purchases), which may warrant further attention heading into Q4.

## Day-of-Week Trends

Saturday was the strongest day with 34 purchases, while Wednesday saw a slight dip. This could potentially indicate shifting weekend shopping behavior.

## Recommendations

- Continue monitoring performance across all campaigns
- Keep an eye on tablet conversion rate
- Explore opportunities to further optimize ahead of the holiday season

## Looking Ahead

Next update on Monday, Oct 19. Overall, a solid week ahead of the busy season!
