# Mossgate Plumbing & Drain – Google Ads Monthly Performance Report

## September 2026

### Executive Summary

September was an important month for the account as we continued to navigate a shifting competitive landscape heading into the fall season. Overall performance remained solid, with the account generating 228 leads (172 phone calls and 56 form fills) on $11,860 in spend. While cost per lead increased to $52.02 from $47.36 in August, a 9.8% increase, we believe the account remains well positioned, and we will continue to monitor efficiency closely.

### Key Highlights

- Total spend: $11,860 (+8.4% MoM)
- Total leads: 228 (-1.3% MoM)
- Cost per lead: $52.02 (+9.8% MoM)
- Booked jobs (per your CRM): 102 vs. 104 in August
- Emergency Plumbing continued to be the top-performing campaign with 104 leads

### Insights & Observations

Water Heater saw an increase in cost per lead this month. Average CPC rose to $14.20 from $11.09, while conversion rate remained relatively stable at 15.1%, which suggests the increase was driven primarily by click costs rather than on-site performance. This is likely due to increased competition as homeowners begin preparing for colder weather, although further analysis is needed to confirm. Search lost impression share (rank) on this campaign also increased to 31% from 19%.

### Next Steps & Recommendations

- Continue to optimize bids and keywords across all campaigns to drive further improvements in efficiency
- Continue to monitor Water Heater performance closely and adjust as needed
- Explore opportunities to expand reach in high-performing service areas
- Consider moving Water Heater from Maximize Conversions to a target CPA of $70
- Test new ad copy variations to improve CTR
- Continue to leverage negative keywords to reduce wasted spend

We look forward to continuing to drive results for Mossgate in October!
