# Q3 2026 Paid Search Executive Summary – Oakhaven Home

## Overview

Q3 was a period of strategic investment and continued growth for Oakhaven Home's paid search program. Despite a challenging macroeconomic landscape, the program delivered robust results, generating $869,500 in revenue on $286,400 in spend. Spend increased 13.7% quarter over quarter as we leaned into key growth opportunities, while revenue grew 3.3%.

## Performance Highlights

- Revenue: $869,500 (+3.3% QoQ)
- Spend: $286,400 (+13.7% QoQ)
- ROAS: 3.04 (Q2: 3.34)
- Orders: 1,412 (Q2: 1,388)
- Average order value: $616 (Q2: $607)

## Key Insights

While certain campaigns underperformed relative to expectations, our top-performing audiences continued to deliver exceptional returns. Some product categories experienced headwinds as seasonal demand shifted, and a few underperforming ad groups weighed on overall efficiency. Key markets continued to show strong engagement, underscoring the strength of the Oakhaven brand.

## Q4 Outlook

Based on current trajectory, we project Q4 paid search revenue to reach $1.2M, a 38% increase over Q3, with ROAS improving to 5.1 as holiday demand accelerates. We expect efficiency to normalize in November as recent optimizations take hold, and we anticipate continued strong momentum through the holiday season.

## Strategic Priorities

1. Continue to scale top-performing campaigns
2. Optimize underperforming segments
3. Leverage first-party audiences to unlock incremental growth
