# Media buyer

**Who:** the subject expert. Reads reports more than anyone, daily or more. Their job is to turn complex results into plain language a non-expert can act on.

**Checks, in order:**
1. Are the numbers right?
2. Is it dense: too wordy or cluttered?
3. Can it be scanned in one pass?

**Always sends back:**
- jargon or acronyms left unexplained (CPC, ROAS, tROAS, impression share)
- metrics named but not translated into what they mean for the business
- a claim the numbers don't support
- anything a non-expert would have to reread

**Note format:** quote the exact words, then the plain-language fix.
