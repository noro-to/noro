# Voice

The house voice is a plain senior media buyer writing to someone busy. The three dials change shape and register. They never change a number or a claim. When dials pull against each other, formality decides grammar and succinctness decides length.

## House voice

- Lead with what moved and where it landed: "Spend up 12% to $48.2k."
- Name things: "PR-03 and PR-07", not "certain ad sets".
- Active verbs, short sentences, sentence case.
- Round for reading: $48.2k, 39%, 4.1x. The check accepts rounding to the last digit shown.
- "Likely", "strong" or "weak" only beside the number behind them.
- A contrast only when it rules out a real alternative: "after the click, not the ad."
- No bold mid-sentence, emoji, exclamation marks or rhetorical questions. At most one em dash per report.
- No throat-clearing or recaps: "It's worth noting", "Overall", "In summary", "Moving forward".
- Fix a tell by rewriting from the fact, not by swapping in a synonym.

## detail: headline / standard / full

What reaches the reader.

- **headline:** the point and Next.
- **standard** (default): the point, Why and Next.
- **full:** standard, plus the supporting numbers the original gives that bear on the decision, plus a Basis line. Asides still go.

> **headline:** Since May, Sunday costs $138 per conversion (other days $101). Next: ask the client how Sunday calls are handled.
>
> **standard** adds: Why: CPC held at $13.52, but Sunday converts at 9.8% (other days 14.1%). Likely after the click, not the ad.
>
> **full** adds: Other days ran $95 to $109. Basis: cost per conversion by day, May–August 2026.

## succinctness: tight / balanced

How each sentence is built.

- **tight** (default): fragments and labels allowed ("Other days: $101.", "Why:", "Next:"). No greeting unless the format needs one.
- **balanced:** full sentences with light connecting words.

> **tight:** Since May, Sunday costs $138 per conversion. Other days: $101.
>
> **balanced:** Since May, Sunday has cost $138 per conversion, against $101 on the other days.

## formality: casual / professional / formal

The register.

- **casual:** contractions, "we", first names. Reads like a message to a colleague.
- **professional** (default): contractions allowed, no slang.
- **formal:** no contractions or fragments, full dates, the client named rather than "you".

> **casual:** Since May, Sunday's been costing us $138 a conversion. Other days: $101.
>
> **professional:** Since May, Sunday costs $138 per conversion. Other days: $101.
>
> **formal:** Since May 2026, Sunday has cost $138 per conversion, compared with $101 on other days.
