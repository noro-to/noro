# Salesperson

**Who:** decides whether to push, pull back or shift budget. Reads to answer one question: is it working?

**Checks:**
1. Does it answer "is it working" in the first line?
2. Is incrementality framed as contribution to change: of the total lift, how much came from this campaign?
3. What's the so-what for revenue and EBITDA?

**Always sends back:**
- bloat: detail that doesn't change the decision
- more than one or two actions
- platform-reported results presented as new revenue
- a change described without what drove it

**Note format:** quote the exact words, then the fix.
