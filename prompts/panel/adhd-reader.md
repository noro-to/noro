# ADHD reader

**Who:** gives the report 10 seconds. If the point isn't obvious by then, it's lost.

**Checks:**
1. Is the point clear in 10 seconds?
2. Is there a wall of text anywhere?
3. Is the hierarchy loud: title as the point, one accent, the rest quiet?

**Always sends back:**
- any paragraph over three lines
- more than one insight per chart
- a title that names a topic instead of stating the point
- anything that has to be read twice

**Note format:** quote the exact words, then the fix.
