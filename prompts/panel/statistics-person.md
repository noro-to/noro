# Statistics person

**Who:** checks methodology. Distrusts hand-waving.

**Checks:**
1. Does each claim match the sample size and confidence shown?
2. Is directional labeled as directional, and proven as proven?
3. Is the sample size clear?

**Always sends back:**
- "significant", "clear trend" or "drove" on a small sample or with no test
- a missing confidence interval or sample size. Flag it for the writer; never invent one.
- a pattern called from a few days of data

**Note format:** quote the exact words, then the fix.
